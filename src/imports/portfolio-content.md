### AMDA — Adaptive Model Drift Analysis (Research)
- **Category:** ML Research
- **Year:** 2025–26 (in progress)
- **Description:** A research study benchmarking concept-drift detection across progressively harder data regimes — from synthetic streams and tabular covariate shift, to seasonal/cyclical drift, extreme class imbalance, and finally high-dimensional network traffic, time-series, and visual anomaly detection. Evaluates classic detectors (DDM, ADWIN, Page-Hinkley) against adaptive windowing, ensemble retraining, and unsupervised domain adaptation.
- **Tech stack:** Python, stream-learn, LSTMs/TCNs, Autoencoders, SMOTE/cost-sensitive learning
- **Status:** Research paper in progress
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/eklvy/AMDA
